# Perks & Combinations

Living design sheet for Better Combat: RPG Additions. Update it whenever a perk or combination changes.

## Perks

| Perk | Type | What it does | Based on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lightning | Charged | Bolt on the target after a long combo | Dungeons *Thundering* |
| Shockwave | Charged | Wave flies out after 3 swings that each hit a crowd | Dungeons *Shockwave* |
| Chains | Charged | +5% per hit, +2% per extra enemy, charge lasts 8 s. When full, nearby enemies are pulled to the target and rooted | Dungeons *Chains* |
| Echo | Charged | Every 4th swing its targets are struck again; counts toward other bars | Dungeons *Echo* |
| Cold Edge | On hit | Hits slow; 6 s of chill freezes the target solid | Dungeons *Freezing* |
| Wind Charged | On kill | Kills burst like a wind charge, hurting and pushing nearby enemies | Dungeons *Exploding* + vanilla wind charge |
| Uppercut | On hit | Sword's 3rd swing or spear's heavy thrust launches the target | Original |

Charged perks show as bars under the crosshair.

## Combinations

Active when the weapon has every listed perk (enchantment or trim, any level). Shown in gold in the tooltip.

| Combination | Needs | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Chained Lightning | Chains + Lightning | Bound enemies get half-power bolts and a 0.5 s root instead of being pulled |
| Charged Lightning | Lightning + Wind Charged | Bolt bursts like a wind charge (plain Lightning has no knockback) |
| Updraft | Uppercut + Wind Charged | You rise with the target, which hangs stunned in the air |
| Frozen Chains | Chains + Cold Edge | Bound enemies are chilled |
| Frost Burst | Cold Edge + Wind Charged | Death bursts chill |
| Resonance | Echo + Lightning | Echoes spark a quarter-power bolt |
| Tempest | Shockwave + Wind Charged | Shockwave bursts where it breaks |
| Frenzy | Echo + Refinement 5 | Echo every 3rd swing |
| Shatter | Cold Edge + Lightning | Bolts deal +15% to slowed enemies, ×2 to frozen ones (which also chills nearby enemies) |

Chained Lightning + Wind Charged: enemies killed by the chain bolts burst.

## Trim perks

At refinement 5: trim template + echo shard → trim's perk at level 1. Same trim again, no shard → level 2, then 3.

| Perk | Trims |
|---|---|
| Lightning | bolt, eye, spire, vex |
| Shockwave | flow, rib, snout, silence |
| Cold Edge | tide, coast, ward |
| Uppercut | sentry, raiser, shaper, host |
| *unassigned* | dune, wild, wayfinder |

Chains, Echo and Wind Charged have no trim yet.

## Ideas

| Idea | What it does | Combinations |
|---|---|---|
| Gravity | Hits pull nearby enemies slightly toward the struck target | Singularity (+ Shockwave): enemies pulled into the wave's path · Black Hole (+ Chains): bound enemies pull others in · Cyclone (+ Wind Charged): kills pull enemies in before the burst |
| Momentum | Moving shortly before attacking empowers knockback / the weapon's special | Launch (+ Uppercut): you and the target are launched · Blast Step (+ Wind Charged): empowered kills give a forward burst |
| Rampaging | Kills grant attack speed for a short time, refreshed by further kills | Another route into fast builds; pairs with Frenzy |
