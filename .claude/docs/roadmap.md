# Roadmap

Planned work, in order. Shipped items move to `decisions.md`.

1. **Decide the trim scope:** keep 7 powers over 18 trims, or one power per trim (11 new powers).
2. **New powers from the ideas list** (details in `POWERS.md` § Ideas):
   - **Gravity:** hits pull enemies. Singularity, Black Hole and Cyclone combinations.
   - **Momentum:** moving before attacking empowers the hit. Launch and Blast Step combinations.
   - **Rampaging:** kills grant attack speed.
3. **Refinement UI and anvil merging:**
   - smithing table icons and autofill;
   - refined, powered weapons of the same kind merge in the anvil at a flat 45 levels, never "Too Expensive";
   - a two-column tooltip with powers first, maxed powers in orange, and faded stats on the right.
4. **Better Combat switch:** a keybind, and multiplayer sync.
5. **Fix the dedicated-server load** (`known-issues.md`).
