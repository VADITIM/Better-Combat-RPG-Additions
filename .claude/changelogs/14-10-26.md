# 14-10-26

Committed and pushed to `master`.

- **Mace timing** (`weapon/MaceTiming`, `client/MaceTimingClient`, `MaceTimingPayload`): ready time ×1.3; a 0.15 s Perfect Hit window at 0.8 of it (1.5× damage), every other swing 0.9×. A press before the window fumbles it; a held key never lands one. The client judges and tells the server at `ATTACK_START`; timed swings hit at full attack strength (`PlayerMixin`). `AttackInteractorMixin` lets a fresh press pass Better Combat's strength checks from 0.8 on.
- **Mace sparkle**: new `perfect_hit` strip in `tools/power_effects.py`, shown by `PowerEffects` over any power effect while the window is open. Perfect Hits throw electric sparks on the target.
- **Axe combo** (`weapon/AxeStyle`, `data/bcenchantments/weapon_attributes/axe.json`, seven `*_axe.json`): right-to-left cleave 1.3×, backhand 1.5×, then a 0.35 s rest (`client/ComboRest`, new `WeaponStyle.restAfter`). New emotes `axe_cleave_right/left` and Punchy clips in `tools/animations.py`, mapped in `PunchyClips`.
- **Level points** (`Refinement.Points`, `progression/LevelPoints`, `SpendPointPayload`): one per refinement level, spent from the smithing panel's new "Level points" section on damage (+3% of the weapon's own), crit chance or crit damage (+3 points). Saved under `points` in the refinement component; old items read as none spent. Tooltip shows unspent points; Field Guide refining page explains them.
- **Power effects** now play each power's animation through once, then the next, instead of switching every 3 s mid-animation.
- Deploy: the profile still had the pre-rename `bcenchantments-0.1.0.jar` (same mod id); renamed it to `.disabled` so it doesn't clash with `trim-enchantments-0.1.0.jar`.
- Build here needs `-Dorg.gradle.java.home="C:/Program Files/Java/jdk-26.0.1"`: `gradle.properties` points at a JDK 25 path from the other machine.
- Also deployed to the `Fabric 26.2` Modrinth profile (Better Combat 3.2.2, Punchy 2.8e, PAL 1.2.8 already there).
